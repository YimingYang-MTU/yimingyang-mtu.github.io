## Executive Summary

Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) offer advanced capabilities for transportation infrastructure monitoring and condition assessment. This **Phase 3 research project** evaluated four core UAS operational use cases across Michigan, demonstrating how drone-acquired high-resolution geospatial data seamlessly integrates into MDOT databases and operational workflows.

## 👤 My Role & Contributions

As a **Research Assistant**, I focused on the computer vision and data validation workflows for the traffic operations module:

* **Data Labeling & Annotation**: Prepared training datasets from aerial drone video collections along the **Williamston Road (I-96)** traffic corridor.
* **OD & Vehicle Trajectory Data Analysis**: Processed object tracking data to extract origin-destination insights and validated automated vehicle counts against baseline ground truth measurements.
