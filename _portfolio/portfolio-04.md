## 📌 Project Overview

Unmanned/uncrewed aerial systems (UAS) offer advanced capabilities for transportation infrastructure monitoring and condition assessment. This **Phase 3 project** explored four distinct UAS use cases and their integration into MDOT databases and operational workflows. 

Field data collections across Michigan demonstrated how UAS methods generate high-resolution geospatial outputs to support MDOT operations.

## 🛠️ My Role & Key Contributions

As a **Research Assistant**, my work directly contributed to the **Traffic Operations** focus area:

* **Data Labeling & Annotation:** Conducted data labeling for high-resolution aerial datasets collected along the **Williamston Road (I-96) Traffic Corridor**.
* **Data Analysis & Validation:** Analyzed trajectory data for **Origin-Destination (OD)** insights and validated automated vehicle counts against ground truth.
